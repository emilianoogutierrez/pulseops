# Product design system

PulseOps uses a restrained operating-interface language: content first, shallow surfaces, explicit states, dense tables where comparison matters, and spacious detail pages where judgment matters.

## Anti-template rules

- No decorative gradients, floating glow blobs, glass cards, or oversized marketing copy inside the product.
- Cards are used only when they express a real grouping boundary. Metrics share one surface instead of becoming independent “card soup”.
- Primary actions are black/neutral and scarce; status colors communicate state rather than brand decoration.
- Radius, spacing, typography, navigation and interaction patterns come from shared tokens.
- Every production screen must include loading/empty/error or no-data behavior where applicable before it is considered complete.
- Tables prioritize scanning; detail views prioritize context and chronology.

## Product-specific hierarchy

The most important objects are opportunities, clients, projects, payments and activity. Navigation, global search, detail pages and analytics use those nouns consistently. Copy avoids generic SaaS language and states exactly what the operator can decide or verify.

## Interaction model

- Global command search: `Cmd/Ctrl + K`.
- Workspace navigation keeps a stable left rail on desktop and compact bottom navigation on mobile.
- Detail pages keep money, status, payment fit and chronology visible without hiding them behind tabs.
- Mutation forms explain the domain consequence, not only the field label.

## References

The design pass takes cues from platform-native clarity and hierarchy rather than copying a brand: familiar component behavior, content-first branding, restrained visual hierarchy, and consistent interaction rules.

## Interface restraint

PulseOps deliberately avoids decorative gradients, glass panels, oversized hero copy, floating-card grids and featureless placeholder icons. Hierarchy comes from spacing, typography, borders and data density. Metric summaries share one surface when they belong to one decision context; tables remain tables when scanning matters more than decoration.

The shell uses a warm neutral canvas, a compact navigation rail and one restrained green accent reserved for positive commercial state. Visual emphasis should follow operator value, not component novelty.
